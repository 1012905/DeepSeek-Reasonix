- 设置›版本›更新方式新增第三档「自动下载」（默认关）：后台下载并校验新版本，没有会话运行时提示你点「重启并更新」；不会安装，也不会自动重启。关联 #13291。
- Settings > Version > Update mode gains a third choice, Download (off by default): it downloads and verifies a new release in the background and, when no session is running, offers "Restart and update"; it never installs or restarts by itself. Refs #13291.
